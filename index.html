Organelle Spotlight Plant & Animal Cells

Nucleus

Double-membrane bound organelle containing genetic materials (DNA/RNA). Known as the control center of the cell.

Key Functions & Facts:
  • Controls all vital cellular activities.
  • Contains nucleolus for ribosome production.
  • Stores and transmits hereditary information.

Detailed Cell Organelles Summary (Grade 8 Textbook)

Cell Wall

Thick, rigid protective outer layer in plants, fungi, algae, and bacteria. Made of cellulose (plants) or chitin (fungi).

Absent in Animal Cells

Cell Membrane (Plasma Membrane)

Semi-permeable lipid-protein bilayer. Regulates the entry and exit of selective materials into and out of the cell.

Present in ALL Cells

Mitochondria

Known as the "Powerhouse of the Cell". Site of oxidation of food to produce energy stored as ATP (Adenosine Triphosphate).

Semi-autonomous (Has DNA)

Vacuole & Tonoplast

Fluid-filled sac containing cell sap. Outer membrane is Tonoplast. Large & central in plant cells, small/scattered in animals.

Stores Cell Sap & Nutrients

Lysosomes

Known as "Suicidal Bags". Contains over 60 hydrolytic enzymes. Digests cellular waste or self-digests damaged cells.

Mainly in Animal Cells

Ribosomes

Known as "Protein Factories". Composed of RNA & proteins. (70S in prokaryotes, 80S in eukaryotes).

Site of Protein Synthesis

Biological Organization Hierarchy

1. Cell

Basic structural and functional unit (e.g., Nerve cell, Blood cell, Muscle cell).

2. Tissue

Group of similar cells performing a specific task (e.g., Epithelial, Connective, Muscular).

3. Organ

Group of tissues forming a functional organ (e.g., Heart, Lungs, Liver, Stomach, Brain).

4. System

Organs working together as a body system (e.g., Digestive, Circulatory, Nervous system).

Model Answer Key (2 Marks): Cell is the basic, structural, and functional unit of life. It is called the building block of life because all living organisms (plants & animals) are made up of cells.
Model Answer Key (2 Marks): 1) Prokaryotic cells lack a true nucleus, whereas eukaryotic cells have a true nucleus surrounded by a nuclear membrane. 2) Prokaryotes lack membrane-bound organelles like mitochondria, whereas eukaryotes have them.
Model Answer Key (2 Marks): Lysosomes contain strong hydrolytic (digestive) enzymes. If a lysosome bursts inside the cell, its digestive enzymes start digesting its own cell, leading to cellular destruction. Hence, they are called suicidal bags.
Model Answer Key (2 Marks): 1) Plant cells have cell walls and plastids, which are absent in animal cells. 2) Plant cells have a single large central vacuole, whereas animal cells have multiple small scattered vacuoles.
Model Answer Key (2 Marks): An organ is a group of different tissues performing a specific function in the body. Examples: Heart, Lungs, Kidneys, Brain, Liver.
